# prani — the 2.3 sweep, 2026-11-17 (v2.3.1 tree → repaired in 2.3.2)

An independent review of the 2.3 work: 2.3.0's second float format
([ADR-0010](../adr/0010-two-float-formats.md)) and 2.3.1's two decisions, a pitch past
Nyquist refused ([ADR-0011](../adr/0011-a-pitch-past-nyquist-is-refused.md)) and a
steady wind's Doppler term ([ADR-0012](../adr/0012-a-steady-wind-does-not-shift-the-pitch.md)).
It ran against tag 2.3.1: 22 suites, 2,546 assertions in each format; cyrius 6.7.18,
svara 3.14.0, naad 2.3.5, hisab 3.5.0, goonj 2.3.2, sakshi 2.5.10. The earlier sweeps
are [2026-08-30](2026-08-30-audit.md) (2.0.2) and [2026-11-17](2026-11-17-audit.md)
(2.2.0).

## Summary

Four reviews confirmed **25 findings: 4 HIGH, 6 MEDIUM and 15 LOW**. Six constant
mutants also survived every suite in both formats. Those were coverage gaps older than
2.3, listed here as C8. **2.3.2 repairs all of them.** Verifying D3 found one more, V1,
older than 2.3: a render's level depended on the sample rate, by 19 dB at 8 kHz. It is
repaired too ([ADR-0014](../adr/0014-a-render-does-not-depend-on-the-sample-rate.md)). Each repair has a regression
test, and each test was run against the 2.3.1 tag and fails there: in both formats
where the defect is in both, in f32 where it is the f32 build's.

- **The f32 build was wrong in two places nothing caught.** Spatial's Doppler
  resampler held its read position in f32, a 9.2e-3 error on a 10 s buffer and
  nearest-neighbour past 190 s (B1). The nasal anti-formant bounded a count by a real
  and could read past a buffer longer than 2^24 samples, and exit the process (B2).
  ADR-0010's rule that time and phase stay f64 had been applied to the oscillators, and
  not to every absolute position.
- **ADR-0011 missed two sources of voice's own**, the canid biphonation and the
  subharmonic layer, so a dog howl at 8 kHz still rendered an alias as success (D1).
- **The Rust's formant fallback was still in place** (D3). One species formant past
  Nyquist cost a voice all five, and the tract rendered svara's default vowel at 8,
  11.025 and 16 kHz. ADR-0013 places every formant in band, as ADR-0009 placed the
  estimates, at the level its resonance has there.
- **Gates that could pass a wrong build.** A comment after `#else` made `gen-f64.sh`
  drop f64 code with every gate green (A1). Two missing twins in `[lib].modules`
  passed every suite and example (A2). Four of ADR-0010's six time-base arms had no test
  (C1). In f32, six assertions fed `pr_of64` an f64 subnormal or maximum, which f32
  cannot hold, and so tested a different branch (C2).
- **A published claim was wrong.** ADR-0010 said each f32 random draw was within an
  ulp of the f64 build's. 25% were not (C3).

## Method

Four reviews, each in its own copy of the 2.3.1 tree:

| Review | Scope |
|---|---|
| A | the format layer: `src/real.cyr`, the generator, the generated twins, the manifest, check-alloc, the dependency boundary |
| B | the f32 build against the f64 build: every f32 arm, the codecs, long renders and long streams |
| C | whether the tests and the published claims can be trusted: mutants in both formats, every `assert_real` budget, every figure in the docs |
| D | 2.3.1's changes: every path ADR-0011 should guard, the wind physics, and the tests and docs they changed |

**Nothing was accepted on a reviewer's word.** Each finding was reproduced on the 2.3.1
tree before it was repaired. Each regression group was then extracted with the
definitions it needs and run on a snapshot identical to the 2.3.1 tag, in both
formats. The results are in the last column below. A mutant claim names its format.

## What moved

Measured with the whole-surface sweep (404 renders, 26 JSON documents, 953 hostile
cases, 64 stream fills) against tag 2.3.1, in each format, after every repair.

- **f64: 161 renders moved, every one at a rate other than 44.1 kHz**: all of the
  intents, bouts, streams, builders, the phrase, the chorus and the presets, at 8 kHz.
  ADR-0014 moves every such render, and D3 those with a formant placed. None of the 127
  renders at 44.1 kHz moved, nor the purr there. D5 and D1 are not in the render set;
  their groups and the hostile set are where they show.
- **f32: 225 renders moved**: the same, and at 44.1 kHz the draws now correctly rounded
  (C3) and the purr's phase (B4).
- **Nothing else.** No render changed its status or length, none became non-finite,
  and all 26 documents are byte-identical in both formats. One hostile case changed
  status: a wolf howl of 0.01 s at 1201 Hz is refused (D1). A zero-length one is still
  an empty buffer: the sweep caught D1's check refusing it at first. 62 hostile cases
  changed their samples, each at a rate other than 44.1 kHz.
- **f32 against f64.** Every status agrees, and the same 17 lengths differ by a few
  samples (ADR-0010 item 4). The purr is 1.0e-5 of peak off, where 2.3.1's was 0.21
  (B4); vocalize's 127 renders are at most 8.8e-5 off, where they were 1.2e-4.
- **Peaks.** 2.3.1's loudest render peaked at 0.786. 2.3.2's first D3 repair, formants
  placed at full amplitude, put seven renders past full scale, a crow's bark bout at
  1.854. Placed at their skirt level, two remained, 1.206 and 1.167, both at 8 kHz and
  both from V1. With V1 repaired the loudest render peaks at 0.665, and none is past
  full scale.

Not in this sweep, and measured separately with a probe of 127 renders at each of seven
rates against 44.1 kHz: V1's levels before and after, the four lip and bandwidth rules
ADR-0014 chose between, and the 49 renders behind ADR-0013's choice.

## Gates

- All 22 suites pass in each format, **2,681 assertions each**, and so do the fuzz
  harness and the five examples.
- `cyrius audit` exits 0. `scripts/check-alloc.sh` and `gen-f64.sh --check` pass, every
  file is clean under `cyrfmt` and lint, and `cyrius deps --verify` is 50/50.
- The consumer floor is cyrius 6.7.2 in both formats: a from-scratch consumer that
  reaches every 2.3.2 path (the placed formants, the per-block blend, the D1 refusal,
  Doppler, the purr, the emotion hold, and V1's renders at 8, 16 and 96 kHz) builds
  there warning-free, and its output is byte-identical to 6.7.18's.
